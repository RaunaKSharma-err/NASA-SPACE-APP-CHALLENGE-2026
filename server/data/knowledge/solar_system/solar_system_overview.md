# Solar System Overview

## Structure
The Solar System consists of the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other small bodies bound by gravity.

## The Eight Planets (in order from the Sun)
1. Mercury
2. Venus
3. Earth
4. Mars
5. Jupiter
6. Saturn
7. Uranus
8. Neptune

## Planet Types
- **Terrestrial (rocky) planets:** Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars
- **Gas and ice giants:** Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune

## Mars in Context
- Mars is a terrestrial planet and Earth’s outer neighbor.
- It is smaller and colder than Earth, with a thin CO2-rich atmosphere and two small moons.

## Limitations
- This is a high-level overview; each body has complex properties and ongoing discoveries.
