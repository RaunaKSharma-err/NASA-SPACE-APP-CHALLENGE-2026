# Earth vs Mars

## Quick Comparison (approximate)

| Property | Earth | Mars |
|---|---:|---:|
| Diameter | ~12,756 km | ~6,779 km |
| Gravity | ~9.81 m/s² (1 g) | ~3.71 m/s² (~0.38 g) |
| Day length | ~24 h | ~24 h 37 min (sol) |
| Year length | ~365 days | ~687 Earth days |
| Moons | 1 | 2 (Phobos, Deimos) |
| Average surface pressure | ~101,325 Pa (1 bar) | ~610 Pa (~0.006 bar) |
| Main atmospheric gas | Nitrogen (~78%), Oxygen (~21%) | Carbon dioxide (~95%) |
| Surface temperature | Wide range, generally moderate | Generally colder, large swings |
| Liquid water on surface | Stable and widespread | Not stable on most surface today |

## Key Differences for Exploration
- Mars has much thinner air, lower pressure, and very little oxygen.
- Lower gravity affects movement and engineering.
- Communication delays with Earth are significant (minutes), unlike local Earth operations.
- Mars surface conditions require pressurized habitats/suits and robust thermal/radiation planning.

## Limitations
- Values are rounded averages; local conditions vary on both planets.
