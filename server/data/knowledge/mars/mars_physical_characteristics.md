# Mars Physical Characteristics

## Size and Mass
- **Equatorial diameter:** ~6,779 km (about half of Earth’s diameter, ~12,756 km)
- **Mean radius:** ~3,389.5 km
- **Mass:** ~6.4171 × 10^23 kg
- **Volume:** ~1.6318 × 10^11 km³
- **Mean density:** ~3.933 g/cm³

## Gravity
- **Surface gravity:** ~3.71 m/s² (about 0.38 g compared to Earth)
- Lower gravity affects human movement, equipment design, and mission planning for future exploration.

## Surface and Structure
- Mars is a terrestrial planet with a rocky crust, mantle, and core.
- The surface includes ancient cratered highlands, volcanic regions, large canyons, and polar ice deposits.
- The planet’s reddish color comes largely from iron oxide in dust and rocks.

## Magnetic Field
- Mars does not have a global magnetic field like Earth today.
- Localized crustal magnetic fields exist in some regions.
- The lack of a strong global magnetic field contributes to greater exposure to space radiation at the surface compared to Earth.

## Comparison Notes (Earth vs Mars)
- Mars is smaller and less massive than Earth.
- Gravity is weaker, atmosphere is much thinner, and surface pressure is far lower.

## Limitations
- Planetary parameters are rounded summaries from published reference values.
- Local surface properties (rock type, slope, dust cover) vary widely.
