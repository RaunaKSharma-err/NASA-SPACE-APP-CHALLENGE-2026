# Marswalk Planning (Conceptual)

## What Marswalk Planning Involves
Planning a Marswalk (extravehicular activity on Mars) would require integrating:
- **Scientific objectives:** Targets, samples, observations
- **Terrain and route:** Distance, slopes, obstacles, visibility, turnaround points
- **Suit and life support:** Consumables, thermal limits, contingency margins
- **Environmental conditions:** Temperature, dust, lighting, radiation alerts
- **Operations:** Communication delay, partner coordination, emergency procedures

## Role of Terrain Data
- Elevation and slope data help estimate route difficulty and energy cost.
- Higher-resolution imagery and hazard maps improve safety but may not be available everywhere.
- Data uncertainty and resolution limits must be considered in decisions.

## MARSYN’s Role (prototype)
- Provides terrain-aware path planning using integrated MOLA elevation data in Gale Crater.
- Reports route distance, slope metrics, elevation profile context, and terrain coverage/warnings.
- Helps compare route options under slope/elevation cost assumptions.

## What MARSYN Does Not Do
- No real-time hazard detection, rock avoidance, or weather/radiation modeling.
- No certification for astronaut safety or mission operations.
- No global Mars elevation coverage in the current verified dataset.
