# Jezero Crater

## Location and Context
- **Location:** Northern hemisphere of Mars, near Isidis Planitia
- **Approximate coordinates:** ~18.4°N, 77.7°E
- **Diameter:** ~45 km
- **Notable feature:** Ancient river delta deposits indicating a past lake environment

## Scientific Importance
- Jezero was selected as the landing site for NASA’s Perseverance rover because ancient lake and delta sediments are favorable for preserving signs of past microbial life (biosignatures), if life ever existed.
- The site contains rocks and minerals associated with past water activity.

## Exploration
- **Mission:** Mars 2020 Perseverance rover (landed February 18, 2021)
- Perseverance is studying geology, collecting samples for possible future return to Earth, and searching for evidence of ancient life.

## Relevance to MARSYN
- Jezero Crater is **not** within MARSYN’s currently integrated verified terrain coverage (MARSYN currently integrates Gale Crater MOLA elevation only).
- Perseverance imagery and findings are geographically specific to Jezero and should not be presented as Gale Crater data.

## Limitations
- Potential biosignature preservation does not confirm life existed.
- Ongoing mission results continue to refine understanding.
