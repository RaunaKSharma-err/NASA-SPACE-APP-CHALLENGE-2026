# Valles Marineris

## Overview
Valles Marineris is a vast canyon system on Mars and one of the largest in the Solar System.

## Key Facts (approximate)
- **Length:** ~4,000 km
- **Width:** Up to ~200 km in places
- **Depth:** Up to ~7 km
- **Location:** Near the Martian equator, east of the Tharsis region

## Geological Characteristics
- Thought to be associated with tectonic stretching/rifting of the crust, with later modification by erosion, landslides, and other processes.
- Contains chasmata, layered deposits, and complex wall structures exposing geological history.

## Exploration Relevance
- Steep walls, depth, and rugged terrain pose extreme mobility and safety challenges.
- Scientifically valuable for exposed stratigraphy and mineral deposits in some areas.

## MARSYN Status
Valles Marineris is outside MARSYN’s currently integrated verified Gale Crater terrain dataset.

## Limitations
- Exact dimensions vary by how the system boundaries are defined.
- Geological interpretations continue to be refined.
