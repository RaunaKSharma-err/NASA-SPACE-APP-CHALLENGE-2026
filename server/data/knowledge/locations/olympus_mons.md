# Olympus Mons

## Overview
Olympus Mons is the largest known volcano in the Solar System, located in the Tharsis region of Mars.

## Key Facts (approximate)
- **Type:** Shield volcano
- **Height:** ~21–22 km above Mars datum (much taller than Mount Everest above sea level)
- **Diameter:** ~600 km across the base (values vary by definition of boundary)
- **Caldera complex:** ~80 km wide at the summit region

## Geological Characteristics
- Broad, gently sloping shield formed by many lava flows over long periods.
- Surrounded in places by steep escarpments (basal cliffs).
- Indicates prolonged volcanic activity in Mars’ past.

## Exploration Relevance
- Extreme elevation, slopes in some areas, and scale present major challenges for hypothetical surface traverse.
- Studying Olympus Mons helps scientists understand Martian volcanism and interior processes.

## MARSYN Status
